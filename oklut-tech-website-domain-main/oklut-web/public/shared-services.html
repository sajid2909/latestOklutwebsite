What’s included

Eight functions, one point of contact.

Take one pillar or all eight. Each one is staffed, documented, and reviewed like an internal department — just not on your payroll.

HR & Payroll

Hiring paperwork, payroll runs, and compliance filings handled on schedule, every cycle.

IT Support

A helpdesk for your team’s day-to-day tech issues, device setup, and access requests.

Finance & Accounting

Bookkeeping, invoicing, and reconciliation kept current, with reports ready when you need them.

Data & Analytics

Dashboards and reporting pipelines that turn scattered numbers into decisions you can act on.

Legal & Compliance

Contract review, policy upkeep, and regulatory tracking so nothing slips past a deadline.

Facilities Management

Vendor coordination, maintenance scheduling, and site upkeep for every location you run.

Customer Service

A trained frontline covering tickets, calls, and chat, on the hours your customers are actually online.

Risk Management

Ongoing monitoring and incident response that catches problems before they become outages.

How it works

A three-step handoff, not a rip-and-replace.

01

Assess & scope

We map what you’re handling in-house today, where it’s straining, and which pillars to hand off first.

02

Transition & integrate

We set up on your existing tools where possible, document handoffs, and shadow your team before taking over.

03

Operate & optimize

Your shared services desk runs day to day, with a monthly review of what’s working and what to tune.
